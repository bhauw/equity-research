# Data to Gather Before Analysis

Real numbers produce better analysis. Gather this first. If a figure can't be found, **say so
explicitly** — then state the assumption made in its place and why.

## Market data
- Current share price + date
- Diluted shares outstanding (incl. options and warrants)
- Market capitalization
- Enterprise value = market cap + net debt + minority interest − associates

## Income statement — 5 years + TTM
Revenue · gross profit and margin · EBIT and margin · EBITDA and margin · net income · diluted EPS

## Cash flow statement — 5 years + TTM
Operating cash flow · capex · FCF (= OCF − capex) · dividends paid · share buybacks

## Balance sheet — latest + 2 prior years
Cash and equivalents · total debt (ST + LT) · net debt · total equity (book value) · total assets ·
invested capital (= total equity + net debt)

## Derived metrics
- Revenue CAGR = (latest / earliest)^(1/n) − 1
- ROIC = NOPAT / average invested capital
- ROE = net income / average shareholders' equity
- FCF conversion = FCF / EBITDA
- Net debt / EBITDA
- Interest coverage = EBIT / interest expense
- Reinvestment rate = (capex − D&A + ΔWC) / NOPAT

## Deep Value / Turnaround — additionally
Book value per share · tangible book value · replacement asset value estimate ·
off-balance-sheet obligations (leases, pensions, contingencies)
